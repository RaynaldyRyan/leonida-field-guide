Roadmap to the platinum

Four phases, one playthrough, about 200 hours. Built from the official descriptions and the PowerPyx roadmap. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so the callout below comes first.

Do before advancing

  • Chapter 1, before it ends: meditate at Jason's house with Lucia (after the mission Down to Earth) — the first meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable step in the game.
  • Save manually at the start of every chapter. Missions cannot be replayed, and cheats disable trophies.
  • Scan vehicles and study animals as you play — the 100% checklist counts progress made during the story.
  • Before the final mission: finish optional missions and open stranger strands. After the credits you keep free-roam, but unplayed missions do not come back.
  • Story choices: trophies named Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess and The Truth Is Out There hint at choices and surprises. Their exact steps are not confirmed yet — keep a chapter-start save before big decisions.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy can truly be missed. The rest of this page lists the trophies to check before advancing, with the reason — not a missable flag.
